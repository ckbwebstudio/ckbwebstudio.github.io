Frequently Asked Questions

Some common questions from potential clients.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. CKB Web Studio is based in Kolkata, India and serves clients worldwide through remote communication.

Do I need to have a domain already?

No. We can help you choose a suitable domain and guide you through domain and hosting setup.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. Website redesign is one of our services. We can review the current website and discuss a new structure and design.

Can you build an online store?

Yes. E-commerce websites are available as custom projects. Requirements and pricing depend on the number of products and features required.

Do you provide website maintenance?

Yes. Ongoing maintenance and content updates can be arranged after the website launch.
